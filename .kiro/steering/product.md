# Product Overview

This is a direct booking website for a luxury Miami vacation rental property - a 2BR/2BA condo on the 47th floor with ocean and skyline views.

## Purpose

Enable direct bookings to avoid third-party platform fees (Airbnb, VRBO) while maintaining professional booking management through Hospitable.

## Key Features

- Direct booking landing page with property showcase
- Photo tour page showcasing the property
- Embedded Hospitable booking widget for reservation management
- Conversion tracking via Meta Pixel (browser + CAPI server-side) and Google Analytics 4
- Thank-you page with purchase conversion events for ad optimization
- Facebook/Instagram ad retargeting capabilities

## User Flow

1. Homepage → property showcase and CTA
2. /photo-tour → property photo gallery
3. /booking → Hospitable widget for date selection and payment
4. /thank-you → confirmation page with conversion tracking

## Business Model

Drive bookings through paid social ads (Facebook/Instagram) targeting travelers, with retargeting for visitors who don't complete checkout. All conversion data feeds back to optimize ad performance.
